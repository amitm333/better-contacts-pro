Questions

Does it replace Apple Contacts?

No. It works on the same contacts, so every change appears in Apple Contacts and on your iPhone and iPad through iCloud (or Exchange or Google).

Could it damage my contacts?

Before any merge, delete or import, a .vcf copy of every affected contact is saved to your folder. Imports show a review first, and automatic backups run on your schedule.

Do my contacts leave my Mac?

Never. There's no account and no server, and the app makes no network requests with your data.

Do I need Microsoft Excel?

No. The .xlsx file opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

How does LinkedIn import work?

You download your Connections file from LinkedIn's settings, then import it. The app never logs in to LinkedIn.
