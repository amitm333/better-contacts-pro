Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Better Contacts Pro ("the app") is made by Wealthbullet ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: your contacts stay on your Mac. We don't collect, store or receive any of your data.

What the app accesses

  • Contacts. With your permission, the app reads and edits the contacts on your Mac through Apple's Contacts framework, so you can view, clean up and organise them. Changes sync to your other devices through the accounts you've set up in macOS (for example iCloud), exactly as they would from Apple's Contacts app.
  • Social profile pages. When you use "Find on LinkedIn, Facebook, X or Instagram", the app opens that website inside the app, directly from your Mac, and searches it for that contact's name (and company, on LinkedIn). That name and company go only to the network you choose, after you've agreed to it; nothing else from your contacts is sent. Anything you do there, including signing in, is between you and that website, and its own privacy policy applies. The app only reads the public profile details you choose to add to a contact. Sign-in cookies are kept by macOS on your Mac.
  • Apple Mail (direct download version only). "Find in Mail" reads Apple Mail's data on your Mac to suggest email addresses, phone numbers and new contacts. Nothing is sent anywhere. The Mac App Store version doesn't include this feature.
  • Files you choose. The app writes a spreadsheet, backups and safety copies to the folder you select, and reads spreadsheets or LinkedIn export files only when you choose to import them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your contacts are never uploaded: not to us, and not to anyone else. The only exception is the social-profile search described above, which sends just the name you're searching for to the network you pick.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
