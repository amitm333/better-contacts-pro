Getting started

  1. Open Better Contacts Pro and allow access to Contacts when macOS asks.
  2. Your contacts appear as a spreadsheet. Press ⌘2 for cards, or ⌘1 to go back to the table.
  3. Open Duplicates in the sidebar to review possible duplicates.
  4. Open Sync & Backup to choose the folder for your Excel copy and backups.

Restoring a contact

Before every merge, delete or import, a file named like "Contacts Before Merge 2026-10-17 154024.vcf" is saved to your sync folder. Double-click it to add those contacts back to Contacts.

Editing in Excel

Click Create Edit Copy, make your changes in Excel or Numbers, save, and click Import Changes…. You'll see every change before anything is applied. Add a row with the Contact ID left blank to create a new contact.

Contacts don't appear, or access was denied

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts and turn on Better Contacts Pro.

Notes aren't showing

Notes appear for contacts that have them in Apple Contacts. If you see a permission message, follow the steps it shows.

Two people are flagged as duplicates, but they're different

Click Not Duplicates and they won't be suggested again. You can change the matching rules in Settings → Duplicates.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Works with iCloud, Exchange, Google and other contacts accounts set up in macOS.
